## 1. What Opiinio is

Opiinio ("every opinion matters") is a text-only platform for
sharing short written opinions — up to 280 characters each — plus
replies, votes (Agree / Neutral / Disagree), reposts, polls, and
debates. There is no photo, video, or file upload anywhere.

## 2. Your account

- You sign up with email or Google. You are responsible for
  keeping your sign-in details private.
- Optional two-factor authentication (authenticator app) is
  available and recommended.
- One account per person. We may suspend accounts used for
  spam, abuse, or ban evasion.

## 3. Your content

- You own what you write. By posting, you give Opiinio a
  license to display it to other users (that is how a social
  app works).
- Keep it lawful: no illegal content, no harassment, no hate
  targeting protected groups, no spam, no impersonation.
- **Anonymous mode:** a post marked anonymous is shown to
  everyone — including your followers — as "Anonymous". We
  still record that the post is yours internally (for abuse
  handling and your own data export); other users cannot see
  who wrote it.
- We may remove content or limit accounts that break these
  rules. Serious or repeated abuse leads to suspension.
