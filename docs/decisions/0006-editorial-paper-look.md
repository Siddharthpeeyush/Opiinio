# 0006 — Editorial-paper look

- Context: User approved the light editorial paper look on 2026-10-28
  ("Yes now it looks fine") after rejecting the dark Waxline vinyl
  look and the dark Liquid Glass look as ugly/reused.
- Decision: Warm cream background, black ink serif headlines,
  speech-bubble cards in teal/coral, balance-scale / tug-of-war
  Agree–Disagree meter. Words are the hero because the product is
  words only.
- Alternatives considered: Dark Liquid Glass (rejected by user);
  Waxline dark vinyl (rejected by user — a music-app look, wrong
  for a text opinion app).
- Consequences: All future screens use the editorial tokens in
  public/styles.css. Do not revert to dark glass/vinyl without a
  new user approval.
